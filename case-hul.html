The Hook

India's ₹21.3 lakh crore FMCG market is in its biggest structural shift in decades. HUL still leads — ₹63,121 crore revenue in FY25, 9 in 10 households. But quick commerce now takes 70–75% of e-grocery orders, D2C brands hit ₹100 crore in under three years, and in 2025 rural India overtook urban in premium FMCG consumption.

The Market Size Paradox

The category compounds at 17.3%. HUL grew underlying sales 2%. That gap is market share leaving the building.

Peer Benchmarking, FY25

The Counterintuitive Finding

Urban brand fragmentation is accelerating faster than rural — unbranded volumes grew 8.4% in urban vs 2.3% in rural.

Urban consumers are the ones trying D2C and unbranded alternatives through quick commerce and social feeds, while rural consumers upgrade to established premium brands. Defend urban through the D2C portfolio; push premium in rural through Shikhar.

Three-Horizon Defence

  • H1: 0–12 months, quick commerce: 50–100 QC-specific SKUs, top-500 dark-store placement, 2-hour replenishment in the top 5 metros.
  • H2: 12–24 months, D2C: 2–3 more acquisitions; scale Minimalist to ₹1,000 Cr with 40% offline mix; an internal incubator.
  • H3: 24–36 months, rural: Shikhar 2.0 predictive forecasting; 200 region-specific micro-SKUs; Shakti Ammas as micro-influencers.

Method Note

Built on Redseer's IMPACT framework, triangulated across NielsenIQ, IBEF, IMARC, exchange filings, Zomato and Swiggy disclosures, MCA filings and Kantar. Limitations are stated in the report: no primary research, derived private-company financials, estimated margins where undisclosed.

How I contributed

Prepared the full benchmarking report following Redseer Consulting's IMPACT framework — source triangulation, peer and channel benchmarks, a three-scenario financial model, a risk register and a KPI roadmap. Secondary research only; no primary research was conducted.

What I learned

Benchmarking is only useful if it ends in choices. Three scenarios and a risk register give leadership something to decide, not just something to read.
