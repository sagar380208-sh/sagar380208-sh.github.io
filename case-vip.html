My most operationally concrete case — real geography, a real competitor and channel mechanics down to the dealer counter. All targets are projections.

The Diagnosis

Traced a ₹12.42 crore net loss in Q3 FY25 for VIP Bags' Rajasthan zone to two root causes — soft-luggage inventory bloat and competitor discounting — then built a full zone turnaround strategy, Project R-30, to recover profitability by FY27.

The Category Shift Behind It

Consumers moved from fabric to polycarbonate. The zone's shelves didn't.

Safari's ~30% market share and 16.5% YoY growth was the primary competitive threat — and the zone's SKU mix was still weighted to the losing format.

Three Underserved Revenue Segments

Each got its own go-to-market approach rather than one zone-wide push.

  • 01: Destination weddings — 4.8 million weddings in the Oct–Dec 2024 window.
  • 02: Domestic luxury tourists — growing +15%.
  • 03: Kota student migrants — approximately 120,000 a year.

The 70-30 Rule — Product Mix Reset

Execution Levers

  • 30-day flash clearance of non-moving soft luggage via Van Melas in Tier-2 towns (Pali, Barmer), targeting ₹15 Cr liquidation to free working capital.
  • Three hero SKU bundles for FY26 — Skybags "Drip" Series (youth), VIP Heritage Nested Set (wedding trousseau, ATV ₹18,000), Carlton Premium (business travel).
  • Gold Dealer Program — the top 25 high-velocity counters in Jaipur, Jodhpur and Udaipur get priority stocking and a 2% kicker, in exchange for 60% shelf dominance.
  • Institutional channel — direct tie-ups with Rajasthan's top 10 wedding planners for bulk trousseau orders.
  • "VIP On-Call" — 4-hour repair turnaround, 24-hour replacement guarantee. A 15% price-premium justifier that a Mumbai-HQ competitor structurally can't match.

Targets & Governance

Weekly geo-tagged dealer shelf audits, monthly hard-vs-soft SKU velocity dashboards, and WhatsApp-bot analytics tracking the 4-hour SLA — the part most turnaround decks leave out.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I diagnosed the zone P&L, then built Project R-30 — product mix, clearance, dealer programme, service moat and governance.

What I learned

A loss is rarely one problem. Inventory bloat, a category shift and a competitor's margin play had to be solved together — and a plan only survives if the field can measure it weekly.
