Movie Revenue Correlation

Python · Pandas · Seaborn · hypothesis testing. Self-directed data project by Sagar Sharma.

I set out to prove production-company prestige drives box office. The data disproved it.

  • 5,000+: Records cleaned
  • 0.7+: Budget & votes correlation
  • Low: Production-company effect

The problem

Which features — budget, genre, production company — most drive a film's gross revenue?

Test the hypothesis that production-company prestige is the primary driver, in an automated Jupyter pipeline.

What I built

  • Cleaned and pre-processed 5,000+ records in Pandas — type conversion, deduplication.
  • Encoded categorical variables with cat.codes so they could enter the correlation matrix.
  • Visualised with Seaborn regplots and a correlation heatmap.

The result

The hypothesis failed. Budget and user votes showed the strongest correlation with revenue (0.7+); production-company influence was statistically low. I follow the evidence, not the prior.

How I contributed

Self-directed. I framed the hypothesis before touching the data, wrote the cleaning and encoding pipeline, ran the correlation analysis and reported the result — including that it went against my starting assumption.

What I learned

Say the hypothesis out loud first, then let the data kill it if it must. Reporting that production-company prestige did not drive revenue was the most useful outcome of the project.
