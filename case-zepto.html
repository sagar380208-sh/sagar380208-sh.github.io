The Brief

I built an independent enterprise GTM playbook for Zepto (December 2025) — a company-wide roadmap to reduce time-to-market by 30% and accelerate Tier-2/3 cluster expansion, anchored to an ₹11,110 crore FY25 revenue base growing at 150% YoY.

Micro-Market Scoring Framework

  • Standardised Tier-2/3 site selection combining income-density heatmaps with 1.5–2.0 km SLA radii optimised for the sub-10-minute promise.
  • Hyperlocal OOH plus WhatsApp geo-blasts two weeks pre-launch.
  • Target: 1,000+ orders/day/store in matured clusters.

Five-Stream Monetisation Map

Two New Verticals I Scoped

  • Zepto Café — a QSR layer on existing dark-store infrastructure. 148+ item menu, 100 cafés/month rollout velocity, ₹160 Cr current GMV, ₹1,000 Cr ARR target. The logic is sunk logistics cost: near-zero marginal expansion expense.
  • Zepto Atoms — a B2B data SaaS vertical monetising pin-code-level purchase data. Real-time market share and sales insight for FMCG brands, targeting ~70% gross margin and 100+ paying brands by Q3.

Fixing the Unit Economics

FY24: ₹4,454 Cr revenue at a −28% net loss margin. Growth was never the problem. Margin per order was.

A three-lever framework: AOV and mix uplift (+₹50/order via personalisation bundles), fulfilment optimisation (route density and batching, targeting ₹35–45/order cost), and monetisation per order (+2% EBITDA from retail media and private label).

The Private Label 20-20-20 Formula

  • Price 20% below indexed brands, deliver +20% gross margin uplift, launch with 20 hero SKUs across staples, beverages and personal care.
  • 90-day cycle: supply & QA → merchandising → scale. PMF gates of 35% repeat rate and a 4.3-star minimum before category expansion.
  • Mix trajectory from ~8% to a 15% target by Q4. Meat and seafood flagged as high-risk on freshness trust gaps.

Q1–Q4 Execution Roadmap

90-day sprints throughout: delist the bottom 10% of slow-movers, A/B test dark-store zoning for peak-hour SLA, a dynamic fee engine for sub-₹200 orders, and in-app end-cap merchandising for the Relish private label.

  • Q1: Tier-2/3 pilot launches; Café scale to 300+ locations.
  • Q2: Retail Media scale; Private Label V1.
  • Q3: Café P&L positive in 70% of sites.
  • Q4: EBITDA positive in 85% of dark stores; CAC payback ≤45 days.

Acquisition + Retention

  • Acquisition: hyperlocal OOH around dark-store radii, UPI/bank cashback co-ops (Cred, GPay), geo-lift precision campaigns.
  • Retention: Zepto Pass lock-in (2x order frequency), WhatsApp/push lifecycle CRM for D30 cohort win-back, NPS-led service recovery.
  • Targets: CAC payback ≤45 days in metros, ≤60 days in Tier-2/3, and +2 orders/month in engaged cohorts.

How I contributed

What I learned

Growth in quick commerce is a margin question before it is a reach question. Ranking the levers by what they add to EBITDA — retail media and private label first — turns a long wish-list into a sequence.
