The Audit

A full challenger audit of Vadilal against category leader Amul across India's ₹300B ice cream market — market share (Vadilal ~20% vs Amul ~45%), distribution strength, and portfolio strategy.

Portfolio Finding

Range without role is just working capital.

150+ SKUs across 25+ categories. The recommendation was subtractive: shift toward high-margin premium and health-focused SKUs instead of adding breadth.

Consumer Whitespace

Nostalgia, health-consciousness and impulse trends point to premium indulgence and take-home — where Amul's value positioning gives it less permission to follow.

Three Growth Levers

  • 01: Premiumisation — margin over volume.
  • 02: Q-commerce — SKU optimisation for 10-minute delivery, geo-targeted performance marketing.
  • 03: Tier-2/3 — asset-light freezer deployment and rural distributor incentives, rather than matching Amul's network head-on.

Roadmap

A 12–18 month go-to-market roadmap targeting +150 bps market share, with recommendations across pricing, product innovation and channel expansion.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I audited the brand against the category leader, built the SWOT-led framework and the 12–18 month go-to-market roadmap.

What I learned

As a challenger at about 20% against a leader at about 45%, you win by choosing where the leader is weakest — not by matching it everywhere.
