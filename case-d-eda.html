EDA & Data Profiling

SQL · INFORMATION_SCHEMA · cardinality. Self-directed data project by Sagar Sharma.

A first-pass profile of a new sales database — structure, boundaries and the metrics that matter.

  • 101: Columns mapped
  • 37: Months of orders
  • 69%: Revenue from Bikes

The problem

A new sales database of customers, products and orders — over $29M in revenue across 18,000+ customers — with no documentation.

Profile cardinality, date boundaries and headline business metrics.

What I built

  • Mapped structure through INFORMATION_SCHEMA — 101 columns.
  • DISTINCT and GROUP BY for dimension cardinality — product hierarchies, six countries.
  • DATEDIFF between first and last orders for the business time span; a UNION ALL key-metrics report.

The result

100% of registered customers had placed orders; the Bikes category drove 69% of total revenue.

How I contributed

Self-directed. I wrote the profiling queries — schema discovery, cardinality, date boundaries — and the consolidated key-metrics report.

What I learned

Profile before you analyse. Two minutes of cardinality checks showed every registered customer had ordered and that one category carried 69% of revenue — context that shapes every later question.
