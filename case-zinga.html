The Brief

Every first order loses money before repeat — payback never closes inside 60 days.

India's premium-healthy D2C snack category is growing at about 19% a year, and quick commerce is already 12% of urban snack orders. ZINGA launched in 2023 with a ₹199 starter SKU, about 60% above the category median. Trial has plateaued at 1.2% of 8 lakh site visits, against a category median of 2.6%; CAC is ₹420 against a first-order value of ₹215; monthly burn is ₹1.8 Cr with an 11-month runway. The question: how should ZINGA reprice its starter SKU and reweight channels to lift trial without a bridge round?

Anchoring the Price

₹149 sits 26% above the cost floor and inside the willingness-to-pay corridor of ₹99–₹199, which peaks at ₹139.

The corridor comes from four Van Westendorp questions on a 1,200-respondent metro panel (a case input). Modelled elasticity is −1.4: a 25% price cut lifts trial by about 35% and recovers roughly ₹2.4 Cr of incremental ARR over 12 months.

Where to Acquire

Quick commerce returns ₹2.40 of contribution per rupee of acquisition, against ₹1.10 on marketplaces.

The recommended 12-month acquisition mix is 35% D2C, 35% quick commerce, 15% marketplaces and 15% Instagram Shop — quick commerce first for price-elastic trial customers who reload weekly, marketplaces for conversion.

Premium Without the Price Tag

Premium lives in the ingredient story, not the price tag.

In the case's recall test (Bengaluru, n = 600), unaided brand recall fell from 84% to 71% at ₹149 — a 13 pp drop that unlocked 2.3× trial velocity, with recovery projected inside four weeks of an ingredient-led relaunch. Recommended line: "Ancient grain. Downtown speed."

  • Ancient-grain ingredient: millet, ragi and jowar, read in three seconds or less on pack.
  • Named-origin sourcing: specific farms in Hassan and Davangere printed on the back panel.
  • Clean-label function: no palm oil, under 120 mg sodium per serve, no MSG, an FSSAI clean-label badge.
  • Matte-kraft packaging: recyclable kraft, debossed wordmark, no plastic overwrap.

The Ladder and the Rollout

A three-rung SKU ladder: ₹149 starter (a 60 g trial pack, 55% of weekly orders), ₹249 family (a 4 × 60 g variety pack, 32%) and ₹399 subscribe (a monthly box with free shipping, 13%). Modelled at week 52: ₹15 Cr run-rate ARR (7.3× the FY26 baseline), an 8-week CAC payback and a 22% blended contribution margin.

  • W0–W8: Pilot in Bengaluru and Mumbai, quick-commerce only, 25% of budget. Gate: trial ≥ 2.0% and LTV:CAC ≥ 1.5.
  • W8–W20: Add Delhi NCR and Hyderabad, 50% of budget, bring in marketplaces and D2C. Gate: trial ≥ 2.4% and retention ≥ 35%.
  • W20–W52: Add Pune and Chennai, full budget, subscription as the default. Gate: trial 2.7% and CAC payback ≤ 10 weeks.

Risks and Sign-offs

  • Premium-brand dilution (medium): the cut may read as a discount. Keep ₹199 only as a limited "Reserve" edition and lead the campaign with the ingredient story, not the price drop.
  • Quick-commerce dependency (medium): platforms can reprice or delist without notice. Negotiate six-metro exclusive assortment contracts with a 14-day listing lock.
  • Repeat-rate failure (high): repeat is 23% against a 38% target. Default the subscribe toggle at checkout, trigger a WhatsApp reorder at day 14, and add loyalty points on the third purchase.
  • Five decisions are asked of founder, finance, marketing, supply and legal between 9 November and 9 December: the ₹149 price, the 35 / 35 / 15 / 15 mix, the positioning refresh, the exclusive quick-commerce contracts, and subscribe-by-default.

How I contributed

What I learned

A price is a claim about value, so the story has to carry it: premium moves from "expensive" to "credible" through ingredient, origin and packaging, not the tag. And every plan needs gates — numbers that say continue or stop.
