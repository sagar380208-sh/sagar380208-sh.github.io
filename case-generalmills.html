Launching a breakfast cereal in a country that largely doesn't eat breakfast cereal. Sizing, a price ladder, and trial.

  • ₹38,720 Cr: Estimated cereal market
  • 8.2%: CAGR 2025–30
  • ₹10 – ₹499: Pricing ladder

Market Assessment

Sized the Indian breakfast cereal market at an estimated ₹38,720 crore, growing at 8.2% CAGR to 2030, with ready-to-eat at 74% share.

The Consumer Gap

The entry point is the convenience-plus-health gap. Neither alone is enough.

  • 25%: Skip breakfast — a time problem.
  • 72%: Eat a nutritionally poor breakfast.
  • 3%: Treat breakfast as essential.

Value Proposition

"Ready in 60 seconds" convenience, whole-grain-and-honey taste, and heart health. The taste layer matters most — it's what breaks the habit barrier.

Three-Phase Entry

  • P1: 0–6 months: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru via Amazon, Flipkart, Zepto, Reliance Smart, Nature's Basket.
  • P2: Tier-2 cities, school and office sampling, quick-commerce bundles.
  • P3: Kirana general trade, festival and family packs.

Pricing & KPIs

₹10 trial packs up to ₹499 family packs — trial at negligible risk, then scale. Targets: 4–7% e-commerce conversion, 100+ key accounts, three-metro dominance before expansion.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I built the India launch strategy — market sizing, consumer insight, three-phase entry plan and pricing architecture.

What I learned

In a category with low habit, lower the trial barrier first — a ₹10 pack and sampling — before asking for a family-pack commitment.
