Bournvita · Knorr · Glucon-D

Occasion expansion trilogy. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

Three brands where consumers had already invented a new use. The strategy was to legitimise it — not to reposition.

  • 3 brands: Format innovations
  • ₹0 R&D: Same formula, new format
  • 5-risk: Guardrail framework each

My most original work. Every insight here came from social listening — Reddit, Twitter and search queries — rather than desk research. All market sizes are my estimates.

The Common Method

Find what people already do off-label. Legitimise it in a new format. Leave the core equity alone.

Each one started with listening rather than a brief — looking for the gap between how a brand positions itself and how people actually use it. All three unlock incremental revenue without repositioning risk.

Bournvita — The Dessert Condiment

  • People eat the powder raw and sprinkle it on ice cream and toast — visible in Reddit/Twitter threads and searches like "bournvita ice cream topping" and "eating bournvita raw".
  • The gap: positioned as "Tan Ki Shakti", a kids' health drink — consumed for its malt crunch as a post-dinner indulgence.
  • Sized a SAM of ₹650–₹1,200 Cr (8–12% topping attach on India's ₹8,100–₹9,900 Cr at-home ice cream market) and a Year-2 SOM of ₹16–₹63 Cr. Margin-accretive at ₹0.79/g vs ₹0.4–0.5/g for refill jars.
  • Bournvita Crunch — same formula in a table-top PET shaker, moved from the Health Food aisle to Dessert/Freezer. "How do you crunch?" UGC Reels and a 1L vanilla brick bundle for trial.

Knorr — The Umami Delivery System

  • A frequency trap: positioned as a 4 PM winter soup for sick days, while latent behaviour wanted an everyday seasoning.
  • Reframed: Knorr is not a soup brand — it's an untapped umami delivery system.
  • Sized against the ₹3,000–₹4,000 Cr seasoning-enhancer TAM. SAM ~₹1,890 Cr (31.5M urban households × 12 uses/year × ₹5); Year-2 SOM ₹90–₹100 Cr at 5% share.
  • Knorr Umami Boost — flavour-first, no thickeners, a 3–4g ₹5 sachet at parity with Maggi Masala-e-Magic. Moved to spice-aisle eye level, clip-strips in noodle and rice aisles, and a B2B line to street vendors and canteens.

Glucon-D — Confectionery in a Pharma Pack

  • Positioned as instant energy for summer exhaustion — while kids lick the powder raw for the fizzy sherbet feel. Reddit nostalgia threads (512 upvotes, 103 comments) and searches like "fizz on tongue" back it up.
  • Sized against the ₹9,000–₹11,000 Cr sugar confectionery TAM — 4.5x the ₹2,000 Cr energy-powder market. A conservative 0.5–1% capture gives ₹45–₹110 Cr incremental revenue.
  • Glucon-D Fizz Stix — zero-R&D format innovation: the same powder in moisture-proof straws, three flavours, ₹2 a stick. From the medicine cabinet to school-canteen checkouts and kirana candy jars.

The Guardrails — Applied to All Three

How I contributed

Independent case study. I found the behaviours through Reddit and Twitter listening and search-query analysis, then sized each opportunity and designed the sub-brand, format, placement and risk guardrails.

What I learned

The most valuable insight is what people already do with your product. Desk research would have missed it; social listening found it. And every bold sub-brand needs a guardrail for each way it could hurt the parent.
