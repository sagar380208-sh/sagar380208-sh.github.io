The Reposition

Ayurveda as science-backed performance wellness for Gen Z and millennials — a cohort that respects traditional efficacy but wants clinical validation before it pays a premium.

Three-Tier Category Architecture

Each tier has a defined job. Without that, portfolio decisions default to whoever argues loudest.

Innovation Pipeline

A 12-month pipeline across functional gummies, adaptogens and RTD beverages — mapped to emerging needs, not to existing manufacturing lines.

Go-to-Market

If the shelf doesn't carry the evidence, "science-backed" is just a claim on a pack.

D2C, quick commerce and modern trade, with proof-based retail experiences and influencer seeding.

Growth Levers

  • +15%: Premiumisation mix.
  • 3.5M: Urban households acquired.
  • Trust: Clinical validation — competitors can copy a format, not a body of evidence.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I designed the three-tier portfolio, the 12-month innovation pipeline and the multi-channel GTM.

What I learned

Giving each tier of a portfolio a distinct job — volume, margin, innovation — keeps premium experiments from being judged by mass-market rules.
