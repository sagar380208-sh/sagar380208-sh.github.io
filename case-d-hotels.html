Domestic Hotels MIS

Category performance · 58 Goibibo-listed properties · five markets. Self-directed data project by Sagar Sharma.

A supply-side MIS dashboard that shows where a hotel category is thin, weak or silent — and refuses to invent numbers the data doesn't hold.

  • 58: Properties analysed
  • 1,408: Rooms of listed supply
  • 0%: List parking, pool or accessibility

The problem

A domestic-hotels category team needs to know where supply is deep, where quality is slipping and which listing gaps cost bookings. The only data available was a public scrape — no prices, no bookings, no GMV.

Turn the Goibibo-listed StayEase dataset (250 properties, 99 cities; 58 in scope across Goa, Jaipur, Manali, Udaipur and Jodhpur) into a decision-ready category MIS — and propose how to measure what the data can't show.

What I built

  • Cleaned and cut in SQL: room facilities were parsed into amenity flags (breakfast, parking, WiFi, pool, AC, accessibility, restaurant, room service, heater), room types banded into price-tier proxies and ratings into bands.
  • Six analyses, one workbook: supply depth, amenity coverage, room-category mix, rating bands, location mix and a Goa property-level watchlist — each on its own sheet, with a README stating source and limits.
  • One MIS page: KPI strip plus seven charts, each titled with the finding it supports, not the metric it plots.
  • A measurement plan instead of made-up funnel numbers: room nights booked, listing-to-booking conversion, ranking factors, amenity-fill rate and review velocity — each with a definition, a data source and a Goa pilot target.

The result

Parking, pool and accessibility were listed by 0% of properties in every market, breakfast by 33% blended, and Jaipur had 3 of its 8 properties rated below 3.0 — the worst tail risk in the set. Goa carried 31 of the 58 properties, with a fix-or-delist watchlist of those rated under 3.5. Prices, GMV and conversion are not in the source, so none are reported.

How I contributed

Independent analysis. I framed the questions, wrote the SQL and the six workbook analyses, built the MIS page, and wrote the measurement plan — and the integrity note on what the data cannot say.

What I learned

The honest limit is part of the deliverable. With no prices, bookings or GMV in the source, I proxied price with room tier and proposed how to measure conversion — rather than inventing a funnel.
