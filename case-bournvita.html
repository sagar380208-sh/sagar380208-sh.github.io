Bournvita (Mondelez)

₹500 Cr turnaround strategy. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

Awareness above 90%, repeat purchase collapsing. A trust problem dressed up as a marketing problem.

  • ₹500 Cr: Modelled revenue unlock
  • 14 months: Modelled payback
  • +300 bps: Modelled margin impact

Funnel Diagnosis

Awareness at 90%+, consideration declining, repeat in critical decline. Customers try but don't stay — which rules out a media problem and points at the product and its credibility.

Root Cause — 5-Why

Stuck between "taste" and "nutrition", committing to neither — and losing on both.

High sugar perception (~6.4g per serving) → viral backlash → an outdated formulation.

Consumer Evidence

  • 78% willing to pay more for healthier options; 65% prefer natural ingredients.
  • Switching driven by sugar and transparency — not price or availability.
  • Benchmarked against Horlicks, Complan and new-age nutrition brands.

Three Growth Levers

  • 01: Portfolio reformulation — Naturals (clean label), Pro (high protein, adult), Focus (students).
  • 02: Adult expansion — professionals, fitness users, 40+ wellness via e-com 45% / pharma 30% / MT 25%.
  • 03: Trust GTM — QR ingredient transparency, third-party certification, nutritionist validation, sampling.

Financial Model

Risk Framework

Taste rejection, competitor reaction, cost inflation and regulation — mitigated by phased sugar reduction instead of one shock reformulation, channel defence, and controlled PR and influencer sequencing.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I ran the funnel and 5-Why analysis, then designed the three-pillar turnaround — reformulated portfolio, adult segment and trust-led GTM — with the financial model.

What I learned

The funnel showed the problem was repeat, not awareness: customers try but don't stay. So the fix had to be trust, not reach.
