Nestlé Maggi

₹1,200 Cr growth strategy. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

Repeat purchase fell from 68% to 28%. Taste fatigue, and a portfolio that ignored regional India.

  • 68% → 28%: Repeat purchase drop
  • ₹1,200 Cr: Opportunity sized
  • 2.8x: Projected ROI

The Problem

Retention decline and regional relevance gaps in a ₹4,500 crore instant noodles market — a category Maggi created and now defends on habit alone.

Root Cause

One national flavour in a country of regional palates is a slow leak, not a sudden failure.

Funnel and root-cause analysis traced the repeat drop from 68% to 28% to taste fatigue and no localised SKUs.

Three-Pronged Strategy

  • 01: Regional flavour expansion — 15 SKUs mapped to regional taste profiles.
  • 02: Premium health portfolio — for the segment leaving the category.
  • 03: Omnichannel GTM — Q-commerce pilots, subscriptions, CRM-driven retention loops.

Competitive Benchmarking

Yippee, Top Ramen and Ching's Secret share the same two gaps — regionalisation and premiumisation. Whoever moves first takes the segment.

The Model

A revenue model linking each lever to the ₹1,200 crore opportunity, with a projected 2.8x ROI and 14-month break-even.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I diagnosed the repeat-purchase decline through funnel and root-cause analysis, designed the three-pronged growth strategy and built the revenue model.

What I learned

Repeat purchase falling from 68% to 28% pointed to taste fatigue and missing regional relevance. The lever was localisation, not louder advertising.
