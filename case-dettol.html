The Opportunity

A market entry strategy for Dettol into Indian oral care — a ₹300 crore opportunity in a low-penetration, high-growth segment where germ-protection equity transfers cleanly.

Sizing & Whitespace

The category's leaders built the barrier themselves. The burn is why people stop using mouthwash.

TAM/SAM/SOM analysis and benchmarking against Listerine and Colgate Plax surfaced the whitespace: high efficacy with low irritation.

Product Answer

An alcohol-free CPC + Zinc formula addressing both adoption barriers — burning sensation and safety concerns. Here the product is the strategy, not the advertising.

Full-Funnel GTM

  • 01: Dentist advocacy for professional credibility.
  • 02: Influencer trials and sampling for consideration.
  • 03: E-commerce bundles with existing Dettol SKUs.
  • 04: Metros → top 30 cities → national, chemist-first.

Financials

How I contributed

Independent case study. I sized the opportunity with TAM / SAM / SOM, benchmarked competitors, designed the product portfolio and full-funnel GTM, and built the three-year financial model.

What I learned

Entering a category is mostly about removing the one barrier consumers feel — here, the burn of alcohol-based mouthwash. TAM / SAM / SOM kept the ambition honest.
