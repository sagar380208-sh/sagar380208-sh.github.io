I joined Polycab India in June 2026 as a trainee on the Territory Sales Manager track, starting in Lighting and moving to Wires & Switchgear. My territory covers East Delhi, North Delhi and Bhagirath Palace, working with 2 distributors.

The context

A strong brand with gaps on the shelf: many Delhi outlets had never stocked Polycab lighting.

  • The problem Retailers already sold rival lighting brands and had no reason to switch. Each deal needed a price, a scheme slab and deductions worked out, which slowed every meeting.
  • Why it mattered Delhi lighting overall was scaling from about ₹1 Cr to ₹2 Cr a month during my time there, so every new outlet added to a growing base.
  • My job Find the outlets Polycab was missing, convince the owners, and keep them reordering through the distributors.

Winning outlets the brand was missing

Converted 15+ new outlets that had never stocked Polycab lighting, and grew my personal sales about 20% month on month.

  • Action Walked East Delhi, North Delhi and Bhagirath Palace to list outlets without Polycab lighting, pitched owners directly, and followed up until the first order was placed through a distributor.
  • Context Delhi lighting was growing from about ₹1 Cr to ₹2 Cr a month, and new outlets were the fastest way to add to it.

Planning beats by outlet productivity

A steady rhythm of 10+ outlet visits a day.

  • Action Planned daily beats around outlet productivity, so high-potential outlets got more visits than low-potential ones.
  • Context With a large territory and limited hours, visiting in the right order mattered as much as visiting more.

A pricing and scheme tool to close on the spot

Deals calculated and negotiated in the meeting itself, an estimated 35% faster than working them out by hand.

  • Action Built a simple HTML tool on my own that shows price points, scheme slabs, scan points and deductions for any order size.
  • Context Every retailer meeting needed this maths, and doing it by hand slowed the conversation and cost deals.

A plan to keep retailers selling

A self-initiated retention plan proposed to the team (not yet run).

  • Action Designed zone-wise retailer WhatsApp groups across Delhi that recognise the monthly top 3 sellers of key SKUs, to build friendly competition and show that new Polycab lighting products sell.
  • Context Winning an outlet is only half the job; it has to keep reordering. I also work with 2 distributors to keep stock and reorders moving.

Skills shown

Field sales taught me the growth funnel in its rawest form: find, convert, retain, one owner at a time.

How I contributed

My own territory work as a trainee: listing and pitching outlets, planning beats, building the pricing tool and designing the retailer plan, working with 2 distributors.
