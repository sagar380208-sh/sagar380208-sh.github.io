Nani's Kitchen

Mock brand launch · Premium ready-to-cook masala. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

A trust vacuum opened in Indian spices. This is the brand I'd build into it — positioning, portfolio, GTM and unit economics.

  • ₹86,930 Cr: Spices market, 2024
  • ₹25 Cr: Year-3 revenue plan
  • ₹8 Cr: Seed requirement

The Opening

"Woh waali khushboo, phir se" — that aroma, once again.

India's spices market was ₹86,930 crore in 2024, growing at 9.2% CAGR. Then, in April 2024, Singapore and Hong Kong pulled MDH and Everest products over ethylene oxide. Trust stopped being a hygiene factor and became a competitive advantage.

The Insight Hierarchy

Positioning

For urban millennials who miss their grandmother's cooking: premium ready-to-cook masalas from recipes collected from real grandmothers, with traceable ingredients. Priced at ₹120–180/100g — between mass (₹30–80) and organic (₹150–250).

Two Defensible Mechanisms

  • The Nani Story. Every pack names a real grandmother, her village and her recipe, with a QR video and a disclosed 5% revenue share to her family. Competitors can copy a format; they can't copy the people.
  • Transparency architecture. Farm-source traceability per ingredient, batch lab reports covering EtO and heavy metals, NABL-accredited testing on every batch.

Go-to-Market, Phased

Launch campaign "Find Your Nani": consumers nominate a grandmother's recipe, the community votes, winners become the next SKUs — the campaign and the R&D pipeline are the same thing.

  • P1: Months 1–6: D2C + Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart in Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore.
  • P2: Months 7–12: premium grocers; Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai.
  • P3: Year 2+: selective modern trade on premium shelves only.

Financial Plan

How I contributed

Portfolio project. I designed the brand — positioning, architecture, six-SKU portfolio, phased D2C and quick-commerce GTM and unit economics — as a mock launch.

What I learned

After the 2024 spice recalls, trust became a competitive advantage. Positioning on emotional authenticity, backed by transparent safety credentials, sat in the white space between mass-market and organic.
