Data analytics

Ten self-directed builds across SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Python and Excel. They include a medallion data warehouse, a customer segmentation view, a Domestic Hotels MIS and a movie-revenue analysis that disproved its own hypothesis.

These are practice builds, not employer work. Each page sets out the question, the method, what the data showed and what I learned.

In my internship at To-Let Globe I implemented KPI tracking and built the performance dashboards behind a cross-channel growth strategy.

  • SQL Data Warehouse: Siloed CRM and ERP sales data consolidated into one governed source of truth.
  • Domestic Hotels MIS: A supply-side MIS dashboard that shows where a hotel category is thin, weak or silent — and refuses to invent numbers the data doesn't hold.
  • Customer Segmentation: Customers segmented by lifespan and spend into a 360-degree reporting view.
  • HR Analytics Dashboard: An end-to-end HR dashboard — from requirements and mock-ups to a published Tableau Public build.
  • Power BI Star Schema: A chaotic many-to-many model rebuilt as a clean galaxy schema — without breaking the business metrics.
  • Movie Revenue Correlation: I set out to prove production-company prestige drives box office. The data disproved it.
  • COVID-19 Exploration & Dashboard: A global dataset explored in SQL, then handed to a public Tableau dashboard with forecasting.
  • Sales Performance & YoY: A two-view executive dashboard where any year can become "the current year".
  • Nashville Housing Cleaning: A messy property dataset made usable for market analysis.
  • EDA & Data Profiling: A first-pass profile of a new sales database — structure, boundaries and the metrics that matter.
