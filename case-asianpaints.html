Asian Paints

Home Care 360 · Category expansion. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

Share falling, margins compressing, and a product bought once every seven years. The pivot from product company to services platform.

  • 59% → 52%: Share erosion diagnosed
  • −365 bps: EBITDA decline
  • ~40%: Estimated cost advantage

The Problem

  • Analysed the ₹5.1T–₹8.5T Indian home services market for growth beyond decorative paints amid category saturation.
  • Diagnosed share erosion from 59% to 52% and a 365 bps EBITDA decline, driven by Birla Opus and JSW entering and sustained pricing pressure.
  • 85% of revenue from decorative paints, with repaint cycles lengthening to 7–10 years.

Why Previous Diversification Failed

Beautiful Homes and Sleek Kitchens show the pattern: operational complexity, fragmented execution, and no service standardisation. Adjacencies kept launching without a delivery model underneath them.

The Consumer Journey Gap

Home services in India is a category defined by what goes wrong. That's exactly why a trusted brand can win it.

Four pain points nobody owns: trust deficit, opaque pricing, inconsistent execution, no post-service accountability.

Home Care 360

  • A full-stack ecosystem — painting, waterproofing, deep cleaning, pest control, minor repairs.
  • Tiered service model (Essential, Premium, Luxury) across mass, urban middle-class and affluent segments.
  • AMC subscriptions — from one purchase every 7 years to engagement 3–4 times a year.
  • Value drivers: higher LTV, greater share of wallet, predictable annuity revenue.

Why Asian Paints Specifically

It leverages infrastructure already paid for — 70,000+ dealers, 143 warehouses, 90,000+ outlets — for an estimated ~40% lower cost than standalone service players, with R&D synergies into cleaning chemicals and pest control at ₹50–75 Cr capex.

Brand Consequence

From "Home Beautification" to "Home Care & Maintenance." Less romantic, far more defensible — a brand people think about twice a decade becomes one they deal with every quarter.

How I contributed

Independent case study. I sized the market, diagnosed share and margin erosion, mapped the consumer journey and built the Home Care 360 pivot and its tiered service model.

What I learned

A category leader's biggest risk can sit inside its own success: 85% of revenue in one slowing category. The pivot turns a purchase made once in seven to ten years into a recurring relationship, using assets already paid for.
