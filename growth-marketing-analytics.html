Growth & marketing analytics

Where does the funnel leak, what does a customer cost, and which channel returns most per rupee? These cases work through those questions for consumer and quick-commerce businesses.

All of them are independent case studies, not client work. Forward-looking numbers are my estimates or projections.

At Polycab India I converted 15+ outlets that had never stocked Polycab lighting, and my personal sales grew about 20% month on month. At To-Let Globe a cross-channel growth strategy I led with a three-person team brought +105% brand reach and +60% qualified leads.

  • Polycab India: Converted 15+ new outlets in Delhi and grew my sales ~20% a month by finding where Polycab was missing.
  • Blinkit: Hold the ₹500 Cr marketing budget flat, move paid acquisition into retention, and free about ₹1,200 Cr of GMV — a modelled growth-marketing case for India's quick-commerce leader.
  • Zepto: An independent enterprise GTM playbook: cut time-to-market 30%, scale into Tier-2/3, and reach EBITDA breakeven across five monetisation streams.
  • ZINGA: Reprice the starter SKU to ₹149, reweight acquisition towards quick commerce and keep the ingredient story: a pricing and GTM case for a premium D2C snack brand, modelled at ₹15 Cr ARR by week 52.
  • Bournvita · Knorr · Glucon-D: Three brands where consumers had already invented a new use. The strategy was to legitimise it — not to reposition.
  • Nestlé Maggi: Repeat purchase fell from 68% to 28%. Taste fatigue, and a portfolio that ignored regional India.
  • Bournvita (Mondelez): Awareness above 90%, repeat purchase collapsing. A trust problem dressed up as a marketing problem.
  • India Cleanliness Movement: Turning civic duty into a self-sustaining status game — a creator-economy engine that works even if people are selfish.
  • Customer Segmentation: Customers segmented by lifespan and spend into a 360-degree reporting view.
