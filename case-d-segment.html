Customer Segmentation

SQL · LAG · running totals · gold-layer views. Self-directed data project by Sagar Sharma.

Customers segmented by lifespan and spend into a 360-degree reporting view.

  • 3: Segments: VIP / Regular / New
  • 14,000: Customers in "New"
  • YoY: via LAG

The problem

Sales tracking existed; behavioural insight didn't.

Segment customers and products by spending behaviour over time.

What I built

  • Running monthly totals and moving averages with SUM/AVG window functions.
  • Year-over-year comparison with LAG.
  • CASE logic segmenting customers into VIP, Regular and New by lifespan and spend thresholds; recency, AOV and monthly spend in one gold-layer view.
  • One view for the business: recency, average order value and monthly spend consolidated into a permanent Gold-layer customer report that Tableau and Power BI can read directly.

The result

"New" was the largest segment at 14,000 customers — enabling lifecycle-stage targeting.

How I contributed

Self-directed. I defined the segmentation logic and thresholds, built the running-total and year-over-year analysis, and packaged the result as a permanent reporting view.

What I learned

Segmentation turns a customer list into a plan. Finding that "New" was the largest group by far meant lifecycle-stage targeting mattered more than chasing the top spenders.
