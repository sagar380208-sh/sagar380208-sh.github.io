Business & strategy analysis

Profit trees, EBITDA bridges, zone P&L diagnosis, market sizing, scenario models and integration roadmaps, built around one question: what should the business do next?

All of them are independent case studies, not client work. Forward-looking numbers are my estimates or projections.

In the CEO's Office at Hanbai Consultancy I self-initiated a go-to-market strategy and presented it to a 5–7 member board review, which endorsed it to proceed if the product launched.

  • Vodafone Idea (Vi): Close half the ₹69 ARPU gap to Airtel, take cost out of the network and free working capital — about ₹8,500 Cr of modelled EBITDA by FY29. A 24-month diagnostic written for a CFO office.
  • VIP Bags: A ₹12.42 Cr quarterly loss, traced to inventory bloat and a product mix that missed a category shift. A full zone recovery plan.
  • Eternal × Blinkit: Four years after a ₹4,447 Cr all-stock deal, how does Blinkit turn a scale lead into a margin moat? A 12-month integration playbook, written top-down in the Minto pyramid style.
  • Ather Energy: How a premium EV brand opens India's Tier-2/3 cities without diluting what it is worth in the metros. A board-style expansion case, modelled at ₹4,800 Cr of incremental FY31 revenue.
  • Hindustan Unilever: The leader grows at 2% while the market grows at 17%. A three-horizon defence against channel, D2C and rural disruption.
  • Asian Paints: Share falling, margins compressing, and a product bought once every seven years. The pivot from product company to services platform.
  • Motul India: A premium specialist in a market defended by distribution moats and price wars. Don't fight for the mass — own the specialism.
