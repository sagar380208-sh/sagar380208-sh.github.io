Tier 2/3 cluster expansion. Independent case study, not client work by Sagar Sharma.

An asset-light partner model to reach small-town India without building the infrastructure to serve it.

  • 95%: Target fill rate
  • >65%: Lens attach target
  • ₹3.8L: Target store productivity / mo

The Model

A cluster-based expansion into Tier 2/3 cities through an asset-light partner model — growth without the capital intensity of owned infrastructure.

Channel Architecture

In small-town optical retail, availability is the whole game.

Authorised partners + regional distributors + micro-warehousing, designed for a 95% fill rate and 48–72 hour turnaround.

Product & Pricing

Frame-lens bundling to push lens attach above 65% while lifting retailer margins to 35–40%. The retailer economics have to work first — they are the distribution.

90-Day Execution Playbook

  • 01: Distributor onboarding.
  • 02: Retail activation.
  • 03: Sell-out acceleration via eye camps and digital footfall drivers.

KPI Framework

How I contributed

Independent case study. I designed the cluster-based expansion model, channel architecture, pricing and bundling logic, 90-day playbook and KPI framework.

What I learned

An asset-light partner model works only if the economics work for the partner first: retailer margin and fill rate before store count.
